# Readings

The articles themselves are not in this repository. Most are full-text from
paywalled publications and cannot be redistributed.

What is here: the citation for each reading, and, for most of them, the
course's own annotations, each quoting the phrase it was attached to so you can
find the spot in your own copy. The annotations are the part that connects an
article to the rest of the course, and they are the part worth having.

**Notes** is how many annotations a reading carries. Some carry none: they were
assigned and discussed but never marked up, and the file holds only the
citation. Those are the ones where you are on your own.
