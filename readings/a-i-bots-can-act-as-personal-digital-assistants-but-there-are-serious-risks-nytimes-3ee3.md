## 1. "pay 24,000 Swiss francs"

This is a vivid example of what happens when AI systems act autonomously without adequate human oversight. The agent was given a goal but made its own judgment about how to achieve it -- including committing to a financial obligation. What guardrails should exist when AI systems can take real-world actions?

## 2. "reports"

Hallucination is already a serious problem in chatbots, but in agents the stakes are much higher. A chatbot that hallucinates gives you wrong information; an agent that hallucinates might send wrong emails, delete files, or commit you to financial obligations based on fabricated details.

## 3. "identifying patterns in vast amounts of digital data"

This is the core mechanism behind large language models: they learn statistical patterns from training data rather than being explicitly programmed with rules. This is why agents can be surprisingly capable and surprisingly unpredictable at the same time.
