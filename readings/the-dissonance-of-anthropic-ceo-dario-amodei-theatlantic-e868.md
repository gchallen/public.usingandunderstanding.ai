## 1. ""country of geniuses,""

Amodei's vision assumes that intelligence scales the way he describes — more parameters and more data yield Nobel-caliber reasoning. You've seen how neural networks actually work. The relationship between scale and capability is empirical, not guaranteed. The "country of geniuses" is an extrapolation, not a certainty.

## 2. "they'd already forfeited it."

Andersen's key insight is about leverage timing. The scientists had maximum influence *before* the technology existed. Once it worked, decisions moved to institutions with power — military, government — not the people who understood it best. If this pattern holds for AI, the window for technical people to shape its trajectory may be shorter than they think.
