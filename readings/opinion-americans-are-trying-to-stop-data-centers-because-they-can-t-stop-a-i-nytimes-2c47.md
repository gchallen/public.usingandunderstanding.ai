## 1. "nuclear weapons."

This nuclear analogy comes up in several of this week's readings — Dwarkesh Patel pushes back on it, and Ross Andersen's piece on Dario Amodei traces it in detail. Pay attention to where the analogy holds and where it breaks down.

## 2. "black boxes"

You've actually looked inside these "black boxes." The neural networks you trained have weights, activation functions, and training processes you can inspect. The opacity Wallace-Wells describes isn't inherent to the technology — it's a choice about who gets access to understand it.
