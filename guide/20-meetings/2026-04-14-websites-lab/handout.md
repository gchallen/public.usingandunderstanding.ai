## Before class

### Creating Websites

#### Today's Plan

Today you'll use AI to build something many people assume you need to know how to code to build: a website. It could be a site that solves a problem, something useful for you or your friends, a personal page, a game, or anything else you can imagine.

You'll brainstorm what you want to build, share ideas, build it with an AI site builder, and close with a gallery where we'll look at what everyone made. You won't write a single line of HTML, CSS, or JavaScript. The AI builds it; your job is to decide what you want and give it good instructions.

---

## Activity

### Stage 1: Brainstorming

#### What kind of website do you want to build?

Think about a website you'd actually want to exist. It doesn't have to be about you. Some ideas:

- **Something that solves a problem** — splitting costs with roommates, picking where to eat, tracking who owes what in a group
- **Something useful for a group you're part of** — a club info page, a resource list for a study group, a schedule for a shared space
- **A quiz, game, or interactive thing** — a personality quiz, a trivia game, a "which X are you?" generator
- **A hobby or interest site** — something you're into (music, sports, a game, a collection)
- **A review or recommendation site** — books, movies, restaurants, anything with opinions
- **A personal landing page or mini portfolio** — who you are, what you make, a few links
- **Something weird or fun** — a fake business, a fan shrine, an elaborate joke, a tribute to something niche

You're going to build this in about 35 minutes with an AI assistant, so keep the scope small. A few pages at most. Not a whole app.

### Stage 3: Build Your Site

Open the site builder your instructor named and start a new project. Describe what you want to build — not how to build it.

**Getting started:**
1. Sign in to the builder (create an account if you don't have one)
2. Start a new project and choose the AI-assisted option
3. Describe your website in natural language: *"Build me a site that ..."*
4. Let it generate the site, then iterate: ask for changes, new sections, different colors, etc.

**Tips for working with the AI agent:**
- **Describe outcomes, not code.** Say "I want a hero section with my name and a tagline" not "add a div with class hero".
- **Iterate in small steps.** Ask for one change at a time and look at the result.
- **Preview often.** Most builders show a live preview. Check it after every change.
- **Re-describe when you're stuck.** If the agent is taking your site in a weird direction, stop and describe the whole thing again from scratch.
- **Don't overthink the content.** Placeholder text and emoji are fine. You can polish later if there's time.

When your site is working, publish it. The builder will give you a public URL. You'll submit it below.
